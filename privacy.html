Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Your songs stay on your device

Stemroom separates songs on your own device. The audio you add, the stems it produces and your loops and settings are stored in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage). They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting a song in the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes it.

What is downloaded

The first time you separate a song, your browser downloads the separation model (about 120 to 170 MB) from this site and keeps a copy for next time. If you paste a link to an audio file, your browser fetches that file directly from the address you gave.

Analytics

Stemroom does not use analytics or tracking scripts of its own, and fonts are served from this site.

Ads

Stemroom is free and paid for by ads. When ads are switched on, they are shown on the song library and add-song screens only, never in the player. Ads are provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technology to show and measure ads, and to personalise them where you allow it. Stemroom asks before loading any ad code. If you choose "No ads", no Google ad code is loaded.

You can change your choice at any time with "Ad choices" at the bottom of the song library. You can also manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com, and read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may also see a consent message from Google. Your choice there applies to ads on this site.

Using songs

Use songs you own or have permission to use. Stemroom is for practice.
